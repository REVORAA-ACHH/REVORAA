REVORAA UNBOX MORE THAN JUST PRODUCTS
01 / The idea

One box.
More purpose.

Traditional delivery packaging often has a very short useful life. REVORAA explores a packaging system designed around reuse, easy handling and a second purpose.

01

Deliver

Protect the product during online delivery with a familiar, practical box format.

02

Transform

After opening, the packaging can be reconfigured instead of immediately becoming waste.

03

Reuse

Give the box another useful role at home, in a hostel, at college or at work.

02 / How it works

From delivery
to second life.

01 — RECEIVEOpen the delivery normally and keep the REVORAA packaging instead of throwing it away.
02 — TRANSFORMFollow the printed fold/lock guidance or scan the QR code for the reuse instructions.
03 — REUSEUse the transformed box as the intended secondary organiser/storage solution.
